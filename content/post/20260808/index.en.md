We posted an updated preprint, *Mind the gap: quantifying population–individual gap in depressive symptom dynamics through energy landscapes* (mindGAP), on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726729v2) (doi: [10.64898/2026.05.20.726729](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.20.726729)).

We propose mindGAP, a hierarchical variational Bayesian framework that estimates population- and individual-level psychiatric energy landscapes from longitudinal questionnaire data. Applied to time-series PHQ-9 data, individual landscapes often diverge from the shared population structure, and this individual–population landscape divergence is associated not only with depressive severity but also with modern-type depression-related traits (TACS-22) and interpersonal sensitivity–self traits (IPS-22).
