We posted an updated preprint, *Mind the gap: quantifying population–individual gap in depressive symptom dynamics through energy landscapes* (mindGAP), on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726729v2) (doi: [10.64898/2026.05.20.726729](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.20.726729)).

We propose mindGAP, a mathematical framework that estimates psychological states (Mind)—both the externally shared population-level state and the internal individual-level state—from questionnaire data as energy landscapes, and quantifies the difference between them (Gap). Applied to time-series PHQ-9 data, individual landscapes often diverge from the shared population structure, and this gap is associated not only with depressive severity but also with modern-type depression-related traits (TACS-22) and interpersonal sensitivity–self traits (IPS-22).
