We posted a preprint, *Decoding Anadara shell morphology with deep learning*, on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.17.719170v1) (doi: [10.64898/2026.04.17.719170](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.17.719170)).

Using Morpho-VAE—a deep generative framework that combines a variational autoencoder with a supervised classifier—we analyzed shell images of five *Anadara* species. Morpho-VAE outperforms conventional landmark-based and elliptic Fourier approaches in species classification, embedding shells into a low-dimensional space where species form distinct clusters. A patch-masking assay further highlights the hinge line as a shared morphological marker and identifies species-specific regions near the umbo and anterior ventral margin, showing that species-defining morphological patterns can be extracted automatically from raw images.
