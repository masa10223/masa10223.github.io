We posted a preprint, *Computational model for Rumination as Self-Supervised Learning in a World Model*, on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.22.768285v1) (doi: [10.64898/2026.11.22.768285](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.22.768285)).

Building on the Social Defeat Stress paradigm, we propose a computational account in which defeat experiences first form threat beliefs, and subsequent rumination updates threat perception through recursive, self-supervised learning in a world model, drawing on the reverse free-energy principle framework developed in the Honda lab. By varying the rumination gain, the same initial experience can yield individuals that continue to perceive strong threats and individuals that perceive almost none—capturing stress-susceptible versus resilient outcomes (including individual differences related to hikikomori-like withdrawal) as parametric differences.
