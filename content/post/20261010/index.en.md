Our co-first-authored paper, *Disentanglement of batch effects and biological signals across conditions in the single-cell transcriptome* (KANADE), has been published in [Patterns](https://www.cell.com/patterns/fulltext/S2666-3899(26)00147-9) (doi: [10.1016/j.patter.2026.101638](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2026.101638)). The work was also selected as this month's cover article. A [press release](https://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/journal/technology-materials/20261010001500.html) from the University of Tsukuba is available as well.
